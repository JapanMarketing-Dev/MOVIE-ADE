Verified, not just “done”

Your agent checks its own work against your screenshot.

After implementing a finding, the agent takes a fresh screenshot and asks a decision model one question: does this match what was asked? It keeps going until every finding passes, then reports Done or Not done, with a score for each.

No more “I think it’s fixed.”

Private by design · Open source

Your keys. Your machine. Your repo.

No Ferret account, no backend. Transcription and organizing run on your device or go to the provider you choose; the agent’s decision-model calls go to your key through a local relay. The code is MIT-licensed on GitHub.

  • Keys stay in your OS keystore. Keychain, DPAPI or libsecret, stored apart from settings.
  • Videos are never sent to agents. Findings and stills are saved in .ferret/ in your project.
  • Free and open source. MIT licensed. Read it, fork it, run it.
