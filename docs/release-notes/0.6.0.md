Ferret 0.6.0 — Orchestra editor

You direct the whole orchestra from one place: the All products agent leads, each product has its own lead subagent, and those leads start their own workers. A product can be a business as well as a system: blog, YouTube, ads, sales outreach or data analysis.

- Dashboard: All products opens on a dashboard with the number of products in the orchestra, open findings, items waiting for you, and an estimated agent cost over the last 30 days, worked out from Claude Code's own transcripts. Type an instruction in the box and the whole orchestra gets it. You can also pick a prepared request and send it at any time.
- The terminal stays out of the way: in All products the page you are checking takes the space, and the terminal opens from the button at the bottom right.
- One feedback for everything: the All products agent lists what you need to check in human.md (for example B1 to B5 with URLs across products). "Review these in one feedback" opens each page in turn while one recording keeps running. When you finish, the agent sends each finding to the right product by its URL. Switch between products' pages, designs and specs from the strip above the page.
- In or out of the orchestra: untick a product on the dashboard, or right-click it in the sidebar, to leave it out (for example a website that needs no updates now). It also leaves the All products agent's subagents.
- New prepared requests (Settings > Requests, or the dashboard):
  - learn from people's past requests and feedback, so instructions land the first time, the same feedback comes up less, and the agent's context stays small
  - start every product on localhost: each product's subagent starts its servers in parallel on separate ports, checks the pages and lists the URLs in human.md
  - set up scheduled runs in the agent's own scheduler
  - deep security and test checks across every product and its infrastructure, with a second independent reviewer (Codex)
  - blog, YouTube, ads, sales outreach and data analysis; nothing is sent or published without your approval
- Dream (memory tidy-up) now follows Devin's dreaming more closely: it reads the past week, keeps a dated log of its changes, and lists conflicts for you to decide instead of dropping them.
