Ferret 0.4.8

- The recording bar stays visible: if the built-in browser is ever placed over it, Ferret puts it back within a second, and the app frame no longer scrolls.
- The recording time shows 00:00 when you are not recording, so opening feedback mode no longer looks like a recording has started.
- The microphone list updates when you connect or remove a device (such as AirPods), when you open the microphone menu, and with the new reload button next to the list.
