Ferret 0.6.13

- Infrastructure costs only on the All products dashboard. AI subscriptions and agent conversations are no longer counted; the dashboard shows what AWS, Cloudflare, Google Cloud, domains and other services actually cost this month, this year and in total, broken down by product, provider, category and item.
- Estimated monthly cost from the resources running now, next to the actual cost and as a column per product: agents list the running instances, databases, storage and domains and price each for a month, with the calculation shown on hover and the date it was checked. The request "Record infrastructure costs (actual and estimated)" writes both into `.ferret/costs.json` (new `provider`, `estimates` and `checkedAt` fields).
- Stops reporting a short freeze while Ferret closes its terminals after you close the window on Windows as an error (FERRET-M).
