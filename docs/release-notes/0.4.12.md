Ferret 0.4.12

- The built-in browser has tabs, in the editor and on the feedback screen. Open a new tab with + (or Cmd/Ctrl+T while the page has focus), type a URL, switch by clicking a tab or with Cmd/Ctrl+1–9 and Ctrl+Tab, and close with ×. Links that open in a new window now open in a new tab; sign-in popups such as Google login still open as their own window.
- Tabs share the same sign-in and cookies, so you can open your webmail in one tab, copy a code, and paste it into your app in another.
- While recording, switching tabs keeps recording: each tab becomes its own track, so every finding stays tied to the page it was about. The pen, frames and text notes work in every tab.
