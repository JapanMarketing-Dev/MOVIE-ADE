Ferret 0.4.15

- Word, Excel and PowerPoint files (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx and their macro and template versions) open as a preview in the editor: headings, lists, tables and images in documents; every sheet with number and date formats, merged cells and colors; slides with their layout, theme colors, backgrounds and pictures. Macros never run. Older .doc, .xls and .ppt files show how to open them instead.
- Google Docs, Sheets and Slides shortcuts from Google Drive for desktop (.gdoc, .gsheet, .gslides and more) open in the built-in browser, where you can read and edit them after signing in to Google.
- Markdown files and source views open without the first-time wait: the editors load in the background after startup, and Markdown preview no longer waits for the code editor.
